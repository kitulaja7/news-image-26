One image that sums up Thursday 26 November 2026's world news: 38 stories
across 7 regions — a fuel tanker fire kills 30 on a Cairo highway, the US
unveils new tariffs as the Nasdaq dives 3.2%, Lukashenko offers to fly
Zelensky to Moscow, and 35 more, all dated, sourced and cross-checked.

- **Entry:** `entry.png` (1024×1024, ≤1 MiB), drawn by `render.py` (Pillow,
  dev-time only) from real wire reports of 26 November 2026.
- **Program:** `main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only) copies the
  committed `entry.png` to `/output/entry.png` byte-for-byte. It runs twice
  with identical output and needs no network.
- **Competition:** [news-image-20261126 on make.fun](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261126)

## How it was built

The front page keeps the dense-newspaper frame that the round-3 winners of
the 24 November edition used (masthead, stat strip, region bar, UTC
timeline, 3 features + tiles, ALSO list, world map + pins, DATE CHECK,
verification chips) — but every feature illustration is drawn BRIGHT:
pale daylight sky, saturated colour, one big focus object (a white tanker,
a red down-trend chart, a white passenger jet). No dark night scenes: the
previous edition's dark feature art collapsed into unreadable blobs at the
judge's 512px size.

Every numbered story happened, or was first reported, on Thursday
26 November 2026 (UTC). Sources are named on each item; the DATE CHECK box
lists the previous days' stories that were deliberately left out.
